#### Change in Beds League rules

A proposed change to rule 14, to be applied to the 2026/27 season and onward:

> **New rule 14:** Sufficient chess clocks must be provided. The normal rate of play being all moves in one hour ~~and twenty minutes~~ plus ~~ten~~ thirty seconds per move. There will be no adjournments or adjudications.

Reasons given in support of this change:

* It will result in higher-quality endgames being played
* For games shorter than sixty moves (i.e. the vast majority of them), it will ease the time constraints for those who need to get home or vacate the premises promptly
* A thirty-second increment mandates that all moves are recorded, regardless of how little time is on the clock
