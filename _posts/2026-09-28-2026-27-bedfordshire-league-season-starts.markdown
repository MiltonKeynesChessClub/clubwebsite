The **2026/27 Bedfordshire Chess League** season is about to begin. The club is once again fielding five teams across the two divisions, and we kick off with a derby: **Open University host Milton Keynes D** in Division 2 this **Thursday 1st October, 19:30**.

Fixtures, league tables and results for each team will be kept up to date on the site throughout the season:

* [Milton Keynes A](/results/2026/mka.html#fixtures) &ndash; Division 1
* [Milton Keynes B](/results/2026/mkb.html#fixtures) &ndash; Division 1
* [Milton Keynes C](/results/2026/mkc.html#fixtures) &ndash; Division 2
* [Milton Keynes D](/results/2026/mkd.html#fixtures) &ndash; Division 2
* [Open University](/results/2026/ou.html#fixtures) &ndash; Division 2

You can also find everything in one place on the **[2026/27 results hub](/results/2026)**, or view the official league pages on the [ECF League Management System](https://lms.englishchess.org.uk/lms/organisation/308).

We're playing without any substitutes this season so if you would like to play for one of the teams, speak to a captain on a club night or via email. Good luck to all our players!
