The **final pool** of the [2026 club championship](/results/2026/club-championship.html#final-pool) is now published. Please check your colour and arrange your games.

The finalists are Andrew Bowler, Joseph Asante, Adrian Elwin, Eric Meichel, James Alexander and George Ward. Andrew Bowler, Joseph Asante and James Alexander won their pools. Eric Meichel and George Ward also qualified.

[Pool 3](/results/2026/club-championship.html#pool-3) finished in a four-way tie on 2½ between Adrian Elwin, Alan Heath, Giuseppe Spigapiena and Vincent Edwards. A mini-knockout was played to resolve the tie, and **Adrian Elwin** is the player who goes through to the final.
