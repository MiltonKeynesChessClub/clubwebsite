As is customary, we will be holding a stock take of the club's kit on **<time datetime="2026-08-27">Thursday 27th August</time>**, the week before the AGM. All help will be welcomed and the more hands we have, the faster we can get through it.

We will also be holding an **all play all blitz tournament** after the count to make the evening more entertaining.

Of course, it will be difficult to play a serious match while this is going on, so if you have arranged an internal tournament game for that night you may wish to reschedule it.

Hope to see you all there!
